# Getting support

## How to reach us

[Send us a message through the support form]({{ site.support_form_url }}).
Please include:

- your store address (for example `your-store.myshopify.com`),
- what you expected and what happened instead,
- a screenshot, if you can.

We reply within {{ site.reply_time }}, Monday to Friday.

Before writing, check the [frequently asked questions](faq.md): many
answers are there.

## What support covers

- Setting up and using the theme and its settings.
- Problems with the theme's own features.
- Bugs in the theme. We fix them in a theme update, and urgent ones straight
  away.

## What support does not cover

- Changes to the theme's code, or problems caused by them.
- Apps and code from other companies, beyond pointing you to the right
  place.
- Designing your store or writing its content.
- Shopify's own settings, payments, orders and checkout: for those, contact
  [Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com).

## Editing the theme's code

If you or a developer edit the code, always work on a duplicate of the
theme: in *Online Store > Themes*, open the theme's *...* menu and select
*Duplicate*. Code changes are made at your own risk and are not covered by
support. For changes the settings cannot make, consider hiring a
[Shopify Partner](https://www.shopify.com/partners/directory).
