# Release notes

## 1.0.0

{{ site.theme_name }} is a scroll-led theme with motion and 3D on every
page, three starting looks ({{ site.theme_name }}, Oakwood and Petal), light
and dark color modes, and a full set of store pages built for any catalog
size.

### Added

- Three presets: {{ site.theme_name }} for clothing, Oakwood for home goods
  and Petal for beauty.
- Light, dark and automatic color modes, with color schemes that stay
  readable in both, and an optional switch that lets shoppers choose.
- Five header styles: Aurora glow, Centered stack, Floating capsule, Nebula
  float and Sidecar, each with an announcement bar and search suggestions.
- Three footer styles: Curtain, Big link and Showcase, with menus, social
  links, payment icons, Follow on Shop and country and language pickers that
  also set the shopper's currency.
- Home page sections: Hero scroll, Scroll story, Slideshow, Video, Featured
  products, Collection tiles, Collection index, Story rows, Expanding panels,
  Stacking cards, Horizontal scroll cards, Pricing, Testimonials, Trust
  badges, Logo list, Image gallery, Blog posts, FAQ, Contact, Email signup,
  Custom section and Custom Liquid.
- Product page with variant pickers and color swatches, media gallery with
  video and 3D models, quantity rules, unit prices, installment messaging,
  pickup availability, stock notice, complementary and related products, and
  share links.
- Product cards with quick view.
- Collection and search pages with filters and sorting.
- Cart page in three looks (Receipt, Editorial and Compact table) with order
  notes and a checkout button that stays in reach.
- Blog, article with comments, contact, standard page, page not found, gift
  card and password pages.
- Motion that follows each visitor's reduced motion setting, with an Always
  animate choice on each moving section.
