# Product page

[Back to the guide](index.md)

In the theme editor, choose *Products > Default product* from the page
selector at the top to edit the page every product uses. To give some
products a different page, create a new template there and assign it to
those products in your admin.

## The Product section

- **Look**: **Product page** (pictures beside the details), **3D ring**
  (the pictures turn on a ring; it needs three or more pictures, videos or
  3D models) or **Banner with a buy card** (a large picture with the details
  on a card).
- **Layout**: **Classic** suits any product. **Filmstrip** suits four or
  more pictures, **Long read** one to three, and **Spec sheet** products
  sold on their sizes and materials.
- **Show the trail at the top**: a row of links showing where the product
  sits in your shop, so shoppers can step back.
- **Enlarging a picture**: **Full screen** opens a large view on a click;
  **Magnify** enlarges under the pointer instead (it does nothing on a
  touch screen); **Off**.

The gallery shows every picture, video and 3D model you add to the product.

## Blocks

Add, remove and reorder these blocks to build the details column.

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Product name** | The product's title |
| **Price** | The price, the sale price, the unit price where set, and Shop Pay Installments when your shop offers them |
| **Text line** | Your own words, or each product's **Vendor** or **Product type**. **Link it** points to everything by that maker or of that kind |
| **SKU** | The chosen variant's SKU |
| **Stock level** | A line saying stock is low, below the number you choose. **Say how many are left** gives the number. It only counts where you track inventory |
| **Rating** | Star ratings, once a review app is chosen in Theme settings, under **Product page** |
| **Variant picker** | The product's options, as **Pills** or a **Dropdown** |
| **Quantity** | The quantity box, with the product's minimums, maximums and volume prices |
| **Buy buttons** | Add to cart and, with **Show the express checkout button**, Shop Pay, Apple Pay and the others the shopper's device offers |
| **Store pickup** | Whether the chosen variant can be collected from your stores |
| **Goes well with** | Products you picked as complementary to this one |
| **Description** | The product's own description |
| **Collapsible panel** | A heading that opens to your own text, or to a page such as your delivery or returns policy |
| **Icon row** | Up to three short promises with icons |
| **Share** | Links to share the product |
| **Custom Liquid** | Code an app asks you to paste |

Apps can add their own blocks here too.

The **Description**, **Collapsible panel** and **Share** blocks are drawn in
the full-width band below the pictures.

### Color and image swatches

The **Variant picker** can show an option's values as colors or pictures.
Set **Swatch shape** to **Circle** or **Square**, then, in your admin, open
the product, connect the option to a category metafield such as Color, and
give each value a color or image. Values without one show as pills.

### Store pickup

The **Store pickup** block appears once local pickup is turned on for a
location in *Settings > Shipping and delivery*, and only for variants in
stock there.

### Goes well with

Pick the complementary products for each product in the free *Shopify
Search & Discovery* app. The block hides itself on products with none.

### Gift cards

On a gift card product, **Ask who a gift card is for** in the **Buy
buttons** block lets the buyer send the card to someone else, with a message
and a date.

### Sold out products

A sold-out variant shows a sold-out button. To show an inquiry button
instead, turn on **Show an inquiry button on the product page** in Theme
settings, under **Product page**, and set the button's wording and link
under **Product cards**.

## Related products

Add the **Related products** section below the product to show products
Shopify chooses from the one on the page. The section hides itself when it
has nothing to suggest.
