# Content pages

[Back to the guide](index.md)

Each of these is chosen in the theme editor's page selector. Every one has a
**Color scheme**, padding, and a scroll motion that follows your choice of
**Off for visitors who reduce motion** or **Always animate**.

## Pages

For your about page, policies and any other page you write in *Online
Store > Pages*. The text comes from the page itself.

- **Look**: **Folding band** (the heading on a full-screen band that tilts
  away as the text rises over it), **Section drum** (a turning list of the
  page's own headings beside the text, for long pages) or **Written
  opening** (the first paragraph writes itself beside the heading).
- **Text width**: **Narrow**, **Medium** or **Wide**.
- **Text above heading**: a short line, such as Our story.
- **Picture**: shown beside the opening paragraph.

A **Contact** section can be added below the page's text.

## Contact page

Uses the **Contact** section: see [Contact](sections.md#contact). To create
the page, add a page in *Online Store > Pages* and choose the *contact*
template for it.

## Blog

The list of posts in a blog.

- **Look**: **Ring** (the posts turn on a 3D ring as the page scrolls),
  **Stack** (each post is a large panel the next one slides over) or
  **Wave** (a grid that rises into place row by row).
- **Posts per page**, **Columns on computers** and **Picture shape**.
- **Show date**, **Show author** and **Show comment count** (shown only
  when comments are turned on for the blog).

How each post card looks is set in Theme settings, under **Blog cards**.

## Blog post

- **Look**: **Cover** (the picture fills the screen, then shrinks into a
  frame as the heading rises beneath it), **Plate** (the picture stands up
  as it arrives) or **Split** (the picture stays beside the text on
  computers).
- **Show the post's picture**, **Show date**, **Show author** and **Show
  reading time**.
- Comments appear when they are turned on for the blog in *Online Store >
  Blog posts > Manage blogs*.

## Page not found

The page shown for a broken link.

- **Look**: **Tumbling digits**, **Tunnel** or **Folding map**.
- Edit the heading and message in *Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit
  default theme content*, under 404.
- To suggest products, add a **Featured products** section below.

## Password page

Shown while your store is password protected.

- Write the message in *Online Store > Preferences*, under *Password
  protection*.
- **Show email signup**: visitors who sign up are added to your customers,
  tagged prospect and password page.

## Gift card page

The page a customer opens from their gift card email. It shows the card's
code, balance and expiry date, a button to copy the code, a QR code and an
Add to Apple Wallet button. It follows your colors and fonts and has no
settings of its own.
