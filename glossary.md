# Glossary

The words this guide uses, in plain terms.
{: .lede}

App block
: A piece an app adds to a section, picked from the *Apps* tab of
  *Add block*.

App embed
: An app feature that runs on every page, such as a chat button. Turned on
  in the *App embeds* icon of the editor's left bar.

Block
: A piece inside a section, such as a slide, a question or a card. Add one
  with *Add block* under a section.

Color scheme
: A set of colors (background, text, buttons) that a section or block
  uses. Schemes are made in Theme settings, under **Colors**.

Compare-at price
: The higher, original price set on a product in your admin. When it is
  above the price, the product shows as on sale.

Dark mode
: The dark version of your shop, made by the theme from your light colors.
  Shoppers can switch when the mode switch is on.

Focal point
: The part of a picture that must stay in view when a section crops it. Set
  it on the picture in *Content > Files*.

Look
: One of a section's ready-made designs, such as **Shelf** or **Arc** in
  **Featured products**. Changing the look keeps your content.

Metafield
: An extra field on a product or other item, for information Shopify does
  not have a box for. *Category metafields*, such as *Color*, come from
  Shopify's product categories and power swatches and filters.

Preset
: A starting look for the whole theme: colors, fonts and a home page laid
  out for one kind of shop. The theme has three.

Reduced motion
: A device setting some people use to ask for less movement on screen. The
  theme shows moving sections still for them, unless a section is set to
  **Always animate**.

Section
: A building block of a page, such as a slideshow or a product list. Add
  one with *Add section* in the editor's left bar.

Section group
: The header and the footer areas. Their sections are shared by every page.

Swatch
: A small chip that shows a color or pattern in place of its name, on the
  product page and in filters.

Template
: The layout of one kind of page (home, product, collection, blog post and
  others), made of sections. A product can use its own extra template.

Theme editor
: The screen where you change the theme without code. Open it with
  *Edit theme* in *Online Store > Themes*.

Theme settings
: The settings shared by the whole shop, such as colors, fonts, the logo and
  product cards. Open them with the gear icon in the editor.

Variant
: One version of a product, such as a size or a color. Shoppers choose it in
  the **Variant picker**.
