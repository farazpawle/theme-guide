# Frequently asked questions

## A section looks still on my computer. Is it broken?

Probably not. Your device may have reduced motion turned on, and the theme
respects that by default. Set the section's motion setting to **Always
animate** while you check it. See [Motion](getting-started.md#motion).

## How do I switch to a different header or footer?

Remove the current one and add another in the header or footer area. See
[Headers](headers.md) and [Footers](footers.md).

## Why doesn't my dark mode change when I edit a color?

The dark colors are made from your light ones when the page loads. Save,
then refresh the editor preview to see the new dark version.

## How do I turn dark mode off?

In Theme settings, under **Colors**, set **Default mode** to **Light** and
turn off **Show the mode switch**.

## Can I use my own fonts?

The theme uses fonts from Shopify's font library, which load fast and are
licensed for your store. Choose them in Theme settings, under
**Typography**. Uploading other fonts needs a code change, which support
does not cover.

## How do I add color swatches to my products?

See [Color and image swatches](product-page.md#color-and-image-swatches).

## How do I set up filters on collection pages?

Install the free *Shopify Search & Discovery* app and choose your filters
there. See [Filters and sorting](collection-search-cart.md#filters-and-sorting).

## Why can't I see the country or currency picker?

It appears only when your shop sells to more than one country, set in
*Settings > Markets* in your admin, and when **Show country/region
selector** is on in Theme settings, under **Currency format**.

## Why don't star ratings show on my products?

Install a reviews app that saves its ratings to Shopify's standard product
rating fields, then in Theme settings, under **Product page**, set
**Reviews from** to **Shopify product reviews**, and add the **Rating**
block to your product page. See
[Show star ratings from a reviews app](how-to.md#show-star-ratings-from-a-reviews-app).

## My pictures are cropped. How do I keep their shape?

Product cards: in Theme settings, under **Product cards**, set **Image
shape** to **Automatic**. In a section, look for a picture shape or height
setting with **Adapt to picture**, **As uploaded** or **Automatic**.

## Will updating the theme remove my changes?

Updates from the Shopify Theme Store keep your settings and content. Edits
you made to the theme's code are not carried over, so keep a note of them
and always edit a duplicate of the theme.

## How do I add a size chart?

Make a page with your chart, then add a **Collapsible panel** block to the
product page and pick that page. See
[Add a size chart or shipping info](how-to.md#add-a-size-chart-or-shipping-info).

## What size should my pictures be?

Most sections look sharp with pictures 1600 to 3000 pixels wide. See
[Picture sizes](how-to.md#picture-sizes) for each section.

## How do I change the picture shown when my shop is shared?

In your admin, open *Online Store > Preferences* and add a picture under
*Social sharing image and SEO*. See
[Search engines and social sharing](how-to.md#search-engines-and-social-sharing).

## Where do email signups go?

People who sign up are added to your customers in your admin, ready for
Shopify Email or another email app.

## Can product cards show star ratings or swatches?

No. Ratings and swatches show on the product page, and color swatches
also show in filters.

## Does the theme work with apps?

Yes. Apps can add blocks to the product page and the cart page, and
**Custom Liquid** sections and blocks take code an app asks you to paste.

## Where do messages from the contact form go?

Shopify sends them to your store's sender email address, set in *Settings >
Notifications*.

## Something else?

Read the [support page](support.md) and send us a message.
