# Footers

[Back to the guide](index.md)

{{ site.theme_name }} has three footer styles. Keep one footer in the footer
area. To change style, remove the current footer and add another: in the
editor, select *Footer* at the bottom of the section list, then *Add
section*.

| Style | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Curtain** | The page lifts away to uncover the footer, over a slow field of drifting dots, with your store name written large along the bottom |
| **Big link** | A curved top edge, one large link inviting shoppers to shop, your columns of links and an information panel, and your store name drawn as a large outline |
| **Showcase** | A framed card with a statement strip on top, your brand and contact details, and a row of link columns and pictures |

## What every footer has

- **Color scheme**: the footer follows it, including light and dark mode.
  Pick a dark scheme to keep the footer dark in both modes. A color set in
  the footer's own **Background** setting stays the same in both modes.
- **Transparent background**: lets the page background show through.
- **Copyright line**: leave it empty for the current year and your store
  name.
- **Show "Powered by Shopify" link**: Shopify's own link.
- The social media icons from Theme settings, under **Social media**.
- Payment icons for the payment methods your shop accepts.
- The country picker, when your shop sells to more than one country and
  **Show country/region selector** is on (Theme settings, **Currency
  format**). Choosing a country also sets the shopper's currency.
- The language picker, when your shop offers more than one language.
- The *Follow on Shop* button, when Shop is set up for your store.

## Curtain

- **Lift the page to uncover the footer**: the footer waits behind the page
  and is uncovered as the visitor reaches the bottom. It turns itself off when
  the footer is taller than the screen.
- **Show the drifting dot field**: a slow field of pale dots behind the
  footer.
- **Brand**: **Logo width** (the logo comes from Theme settings, under
  **Footer**), **Show the brand name beside the logo** and **Brand name**.
- **Contact**: **Phone**, **Email**, **Address** and **Opening hours**.
- **Certifications and badges**: up to four logos with links, such as
  memberships or awards, with a **Small heading above the logos**. One
  **Alignment** moves the heading and the logos together.
- **Bottom bar**: **Show store policy links** (only policies you have
  written appear), **Show payment icons** and **Show the back to top
  button**.
- **Big name**: **Show the big name**, **Big name text** and **Big name
  size** (**Small**, **Medium** or **Large**). A long name is made smaller
  where needed, so it is never cut off.
- Columns of links are blocks: add a **Link column** for each, and choose a
  **Footer menu** or type up to five links.

## Big link

The frame of this footer is fixed: the curved top, the big link, the store
name and the bottom bar are always there. The row of columns is the part you
build yourself.

- **Big link**: **Show the big link** turns the whole invitation line off.
  When on, set its **Wording** and where it **Goes to**. Leave both empty
  for a general invitation to shop all products.
- **Link column** blocks: each sets its own **Column width**, heading,
  links (a **Footer menu**, or **Custom links**) and style. **Collapse on
  mobile** turns its heading into an expander on phones. To hide a column,
  remove it.
- **Information panel**: a fixed quarter on the right, with one detail (a
  label, the wording, and an optional link such as `tel:` or `mailto:`).
  Turn off **Show the information panel** to give the columns the full
  width.
- **Store name**: drawn large as an outline. **Hover effect** fills it with
  the accent color, or **Slice distortion** tears it into bands around the
  pointer.
- Up to three typed links in the middle of the bottom bar, such as
  shipping, terms and privacy.

## Showcase

- **Statement strip**: a **Small message** and a **Heading** across the
  top. It is shown only when it has wording.
- **Brand content**: **Alignment** moves the **Logo**, **Brand name** and
  **Short description** together. Leave **Brand name** empty to use your
  store name. **Gradient color** fades the brand name from the color you
  pick into the footer's accent color.
- **Contact information**: address, phone and email, each under a fixed
  label. Leave a detail empty to hide it.
- **Footer page links**: pick the page behind each fixed link (**Privacy
  policy**, **Terms and conditions**, **Shipping and delivery**, **Returns
  and refunds**, **Contact us**). A link with no page stays hidden.
- Blocks: **Link column** for links and **Image** for pictures, such as a
  badge or a photo. **Cover a full row** lets a picture span the row.
