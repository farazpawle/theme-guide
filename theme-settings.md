# Theme settings

[Back to the guide](index.md)

Theme settings hold everything shared across the whole shop. In the theme
editor, select the paintbrush icon to open them. Each group below matches a
group there.

## Brand

- **Logo**: shown in the header. Leave it empty to show your store name as
  text instead. **Logo width** sets its size.
- **Favicon**: the small icon in the browser tab. A square image works best.

## Colors

### Color schemes

A color scheme is a set of colors that sections use together. Every section
with a **Color scheme** setting lets you pick one, so you can change many
sections at once by editing one scheme.

Each scheme has these colors:

| Color | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| **Background** | The section's background |
| **Background gradient** | An optional gradient over the background |
| **Foreground** | Text and icons |
| **Accent** | Highlights: badges, active dots, captions, selected options |
| **Button** | The main buttons |
| **Button text** | The words on the main buttons |

**Page color scheme** sets the colors of the page itself and of every
section that does not pick a scheme of its own.

**Input corner radius** rounds the corners of every typing box: search, the
email signup and the contact form.

### Light and dark mode

You set only the light colors. The theme makes the dark version of every
scheme for you and keeps its text readable.

- **Default mode**: **Light**, **Dark**, or **Follow the visitor's device**.
- **Show the mode switch**: lets visitors choose for themselves. On
  computers it is an icon in the header; on phones and tablets it is a row in
  the menu. A visitor's choice is remembered on their device.

The editor cannot preview dark colors straight away: after changing a
scheme, save and refresh the preview to see its dark version.

## Typography

- **Heading font** and **Heading size** for headings.
- **Body font** and **Body size** for all other text.

The fonts come from Shopify's font library, so they load fast and need no
license of your own.

## Layout

- **Page width**: the widest the content gets on large screens.
- **Page margin**: the space at the sides of the page.
- **Space between sections**: the gap between one section and the next.

## Animations

**Reveal content as it scrolls into view** fades content in as visitors
scroll. Clear it to show everything straight away across the whole shop.
Each section's own motion is set in that section.

## Background

- **Background image**: a picture behind the whole page, shown through
  transparent sections. Pick one your text reads clearly on.
- **Make all sections transparent**: lets the background image show through
  every section. A section can override this with its own **Transparent
  background** setting.

## Header

- **Keep the header on screen**: the header stays at the top as visitors
  scroll. The announcement bar scrolls away.
- **Enable the horizontal mega menu**: a menu item with two levels below it
  opens as columns instead of a single list.

The header's style is chosen in the header itself: see
[Headers](headers.md).

## Announcement bar

One strip above the header, on every page. Every header style shows it
except **Floating capsule**.

- **Show the announcement bar**, **Message** and **Link**. When a link is
  set, the whole message becomes the link.
- **Let visitors close it**: a closed bar stays closed until you change the
  message.
- **Background** and **Foreground**: leave both empty to use the header's
  button colors.

## Footer

**Footer logo**: leave it empty to reuse the header logo. Footer styles are
covered in [Footers](footers.md).

## Product cards

The product card is used everywhere products are listed: featured products,
collections, search and recommendations. Set it once here and every card
matches.

- **Style**: **Standard** or **Card** (a framed card).
- **Alignment** and **Product name lines**: how many lines a long name
  takes. Collection and search pages always show the full name.
- **Image shape**: **Automatic** keeps your pictures' own shape; the others
  crop every card to the same shape. **Custom shape from an image** uses the
  outline of an image you upload. **Crop images to fill** fills the shape
  without gaps.
- **Hover style**: **Zoom the picture**, **Show the second picture** or **No
  hover effect**.
- **Color scheme**, **Border**, **Corner rounding** and **Elevation** (the
  shadow).
- **Sold out products**: **Show sold out**, or **Show an inquiry button**
  with your own **Inquiry button text** and **Inquiry button link** (for
  example your contact page).

## Blog cards

How blog posts look in the **Blog posts** section and on the blog page:
**Style**, **Alignment**, **Color scheme**, **Image padding**, and the
**Border**, **Corner rounding** and **Elevation** of the card.

## Content containers

The **Border**, **Corner rounding** and **Elevation** of panels and framed
pictures across the theme, such as cart panels and **Slideshow** pictures
in the **Gallery** look.

## Product page

- **Reviews from**: choose **Shopify product reviews** to show star ratings
  from that app in the product page's **Rating** block. With **None**, the
  block shows nothing.
- **Show an inquiry button on the product page**: on sold-out products, an
  inquiry button replaces the buy button. It uses the inquiry text and link
  set under Product cards.

## Cart

- **Empty cart suggestions**: a collection of products shown when the cart
  is empty.
- **Let shoppers add a note**: a note box for instructions with the order.
- **Show vendor**: the maker's name under each item.

## Search

### Search as you type

**Suggest results as the shopper types** shows products, collections and
pages while the shopper types in any search box. With it on, choose
**Show price**, **Show SKU** and **Show vendor**.

### Search behavior

- **Popular searches**: one term per line, shown before anything is typed.
- **Search across**: **Products only** or **Everything** (pages and blog
  posts too).

### Sale badge

- **Show a sale badge** on products that cost less than their compare-at
  price.
- **Badge style**: **Sale label** or **Percentage off**.
- **Minimum discount**: hides the badge on smaller discounts. Zero shows it
  on every reduced product.

## Currency format

- **Show the currency code**: shows $1.00 USD rather than $1.00. Useful
  when you sell in more than one currency.
- **Show country/region selector**: lets shoppers choose the country they
  buy from, which sets their currency. It appears only when you sell to more
  than one country (*Settings > Markets* in your admin). A language selector
  appears by itself when your shop has more than one language.

## Social media

Paste a link for each platform you use: **Facebook**, **Instagram**,
**LinkedIn**, **Pinterest**, **TikTok**, **X** and **YouTube**. Only the
platforms with a link are shown in the footer.
