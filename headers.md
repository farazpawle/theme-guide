# Headers and the announcement bar

[Back to the guide](index.md)

{{ site.theme_name }} has five header styles. Keep one header in the header
area. To change style, remove the current header and add another: in the
editor, select *Header* at the top of the section list, then *Add section*.

Every style has a **Color scheme**, a **Main menu** and a **Customer
account menu**. Menus are made in your admin under *Content > Menus*. A menu
item with items below it opens as a list; with two levels below it and
**Enable the horizontal mega menu** turned on (Theme settings, **Header**),
it opens as columns.

On phones and tablets every style folds its menu behind a menu button.

## The styles

| Style | What it looks like | Best with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aurora glow** | A bar with a soft glow of light behind it | A dark color scheme |
| **Centered stack** | Your shop name centered, the menu on its own row below, and an optional thin row of small links above | Shops with many menu items |
| **Floating capsule** | A rounded bar floating above the page; the menu opens full screen | A clean, minimal look |
| **Nebula float** | Controls floating straight on the page with no bar behind them | A home page that opens on a full-screen picture or a dark scene |
| **Sidecar** | A bar whose menu slides in from the left as a drawer | Large menus |

### Aurora glow

- **Show search** shows the search icon.
- **Glow strength** sets how bright the glow is.

### Centered stack

- **Show the links strip** adds a thin row of small links above the shop
  name, for things like order tracking and help. Choose **Links on the
  left** and **Links on the right**. It shows on computers only; on phones
  and tablets these links move to the bottom of the menu.
- **Letter spacing** spaces out the letters of your shop name (not a logo
  picture).

### Floating capsule

- **Footer links**: a short list along the bottom of the full-screen menu.
- **Corner radius** and **Elevation** shape the capsule and its shadow.
- This style has no announcement bar. On computers, search is reached from
  the search page.

### Nebula float

- Search is always on.
- **Bar background**: **None — float on the page** (the style as designed)
  or **Fill with the scheme color**.
- **Light strength** sets the glow behind the controls.

### Sidecar

- **Drawer footer links**: a short list along the bottom of the drawer. The
  account link is added for you.
- **Bar height** applies on computers.
- **Drawer color scheme** colors the drawer. A scheme that contrasts with
  the bar reads best.

## Logo

The logo is set once in Theme settings, under **Brand**. Without one, the
header shows your store name as text.

## Search suggestions

When **Suggest results as the shopper types** is on (Theme settings,
**Search**), the search box suggests products, collections and pages as
the shopper types.

## Announcement bar

The announcement bar is set in Theme settings, under **Announcement bar**:
see [Theme settings](theme-settings.md#announcement-bar).

## Account and the mode switch

- The account icon appears when customer accounts are turned on in
  *Settings > Customer accounts*.
- The light and dark switch appears when **Show the mode switch** is on.

The country and language pickers are in the footer: see
[Footers](footers.md).
